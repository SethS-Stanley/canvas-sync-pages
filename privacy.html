Privacy Policy

Canvas Sync is a personal-use Obsidian plugin. It is used solely by its developer and is not distributed to the public.

When connected to Gmail, the plugin requests read-only access to your inbox (gmail.readonly scope) to display unread counts inside Obsidian. Email data is read directly from Google's servers and displayed locally; it is never transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server. OAuth tokens are stored locally in the Obsidian vault's own settings file, encrypted at rest by the operating system where supported, and never leave the user's device.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI/ML training.
